How to apply.

Four steps from application to your first day of orientation.

  1. 01

    Submit the Universal Application

    Apply with your transcript, a short essay, and a $50 fee, waived for students who qualify.

    Priority: Nov 1
  2. 02

    Send supporting materials

    Test scores are optional. Two letters of recommendation and a counselor report complete the file.

    Due: Dec 15
  3. 03

    Admissions review

    A committee reads every application in full. Nothing here is decided by an algorithm.

    Decisions: Feb 1
  4. 04

    Enroll & orientation

    Confirm your spot, register for First-Year Orientation, and meet your academic advisor.

    Confirm by: May 1
