Applying to City University.

Four steps, one reader for every file, and a decision by February 1 for students who meet the priority deadline. Questions go to the Registrar's Office, run by April Ludgate.

How to apply, step by step.

  1. 01

    Submit the Universal Application

    Apply online with your high school transcript, a 500-word essay, and a $50 application fee. The fee is waived automatically for students eligible for a school lunch fee waiver.

    Priority: Nov 1
  2. 02

    Send supporting materials

    Standardized test scores are optional and considered only if submitted. Two letters of recommendation and a counselor report are required from every applicant.

    Due: Dec 15
  3. 03

    Admissions review

    A two-reader committee reads every application in full, weighing coursework rigor, the essay, and recommendations. No algorithmic score sorts applicants before a human reads the file.

    Decisions: Feb 1
  4. 04

    Enroll & orientation

    Confirm your spot with a $200 enrollment deposit (refundable through May 1 for financial hardship), then register for a two-day First-Year Orientation session over the summer.

    Confirm by: May 1
