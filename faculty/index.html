The people teaching 24,600 students.

City University employs 1,180 faculty across six colleges, 74% of them tenured or on the tenure track. Below is university leadership and a sample of teaching faculty by college.

Who runs the university.

  • Dr. Leslie Knope President Started as a City University admissions counselor in 2003 before returning as its 14th president in 2021. Office of the President
  • Dr. Ben Wyatt Provost & Dean, College of Engineering Sets academic policy across all six colleges and chairs the curriculum committee. Office of the Provost
  • April Ludgate University Registrar Runs course registration, transcripts, and the public course catalog. Registrar's Office

A sample from each college.

  • Dr. Jeff Winger Professor of Communication Studies Teaches Persuasive Rhetoric, the most oversubscribed course on campus. Arts & Sciences
  • Dr. Britta Perry Associate Professor of Psychology Runs the campus's community mental health clinic training program. Arts & Sciences
  • Dr. Abed Nadir Associate Professor of Film & Media Directs the student-run campus television station. Arts & Sciences
  • Dr. Sheldon Cooper Professor of Theoretical Physics Directs the undergraduate research track in the Physics Department. Engineering
  • Dr. Annie Edison Assistant Professor of Public Health Advises pre-health students applying to nursing and physician assistant programs. Health Sciences
  • Troy Barnes Lecturer in Kinesiology Teaches the sports science courses required of every athletic training minor. Health Sciences
  • Shirley Bennett Associate Professor of Accounting Coordinates the College of Business's CPA exam preparation track. Business
