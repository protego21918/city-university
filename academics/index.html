What every student takes, regardless of major.

Every undergraduate, in any of the five undergraduate colleges, completes the same 40-credit general-education core before graduation: two writing seminars, one quantitative reasoning course, one lab science, one course in the arts, and a two-semester capstone in their major department.

Transfer students from an accredited two-year program can bring in up to 24 credits toward the core with an official transcript review, handled by the Registrar's Office rather than by individual departments.
