From advising hold to a locked schedule.

  1. 01

    Meet with your academic advisor

    An advising hold blocks registration until you've met with your advisor and reviewed your degree audit.

  2. 02

    Get your registration window

    Windows open by class standing, seniors first, so students closest to graduation get first pick of seats.

  3. 03

    Register

    Add courses directly through the registrar system once your window opens. Full sections offer a waitlist.

  4. 04

    Add or drop by the deadline

    Schedules lock two weeks into the term. Drops after that date require a signed withdrawal form.
